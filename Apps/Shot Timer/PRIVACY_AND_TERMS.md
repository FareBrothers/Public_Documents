## Privacy Policy

Shot Timer+ is designed to work entirely on your device. It does not require an account.

### Data We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, receive, store, sell, or share personal information or usage data from the app. The app contains no advertising, analytics, or tracking services and does not track you across apps or websites.

### Information Used on Your Device

- **Microphone input:** With your permission, the app analyzes microphone input in real time to detect a sudden sound and calculate a reaction time. Audio is never recorded, saved, or transmitted.
- **Shot history:** The app saves the reaction time, date, and selected start mode for completed sessions locally on your device. This history is never sent to us or any third party.

### Permissions

Microphone access is required for sound detection. You may revoke it at any time in your device’s Settings. The app cannot detect shots while microphone access is disabled.

### Storage and Deletion

Shot history remains only on your device until you delete an entry or uninstall the app. A device backup may retain app data according to your device and backup settings. We cannot access, recover, or delete this data because we never receive it.

### Children’s Privacy
