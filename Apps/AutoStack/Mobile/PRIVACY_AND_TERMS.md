## Privacy Policy

### 1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use AutoStack. AutoStack does not require an account and is designed to keep vehicle and diagnostic information on your device.

### 2. Information handled by the App

#### Vehicle and diagnostic information

Depending on the features you use, AutoStack may read or store:

- vehicle identification numbers (VINs), model years, makes, models, trims, engines, and OBD protocols;
- diagnostic trouble codes, freeze-frame data, emissions-readiness status, supported parameter identifiers (PIDs), live sensor values, module information, and scan history;
- vehicle profiles, custom PIDs, dashboard layouts, unit preferences, and selected manufacturer packages; and
- service-procedure settings and operation history.

This information is stored locally on your device. AutoStack does not automatically upload vehicle or diagnostic information to Fare Brothers.

#### Adapter and local-network information

AutoStack uses Bluetooth or your local Wi-Fi network to communicate directly with an OBD-II adapter. The App may access nearby Bluetooth adapter information and the local-network address and port you provide. This information is used to establish and maintain the adapter connection and is not used for tracking or advertising.

#### Purchases

Manufacturer packages and other in-app purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit. AutoStack receives purchase and entitlement status needed to unlock purchased features. Fare Brothers does not receive your complete payment-card information. Apple's handling of purchase information is governed by Apple's privacy policy and applicable App Store terms.

#### Data updates

When you check for or download catalog updates, AutoStack connects to content hosted through GitHub. GitHub may receive standard network information, such as your IP address, device or browser request information, and request timestamps, as described in GitHub's privacy statement. AutoStack does not include vehicle diagnostic data in these requests.

#### Support requests

If you choose to contact support, you are directed to Fare Brothers' Freshdesk support portal. Information you submit may include your name, email address, message, attachments, and any diagnostic information you voluntarily provide. We use this information to respond to and manage your request. Freshworks processes information submitted through Freshdesk under its privacy notice.

### 3. Information we do not collect through the App

AutoStack does not include third-party advertising, behavioral tracking, or analytics SDKs. The App does not request access to your precise location, contacts, calendars, microphone, or photo library for its core diagnostic functions. We do not sell vehicle information or personal information.

### 4. How information is used

Information handled by AutoStack is used to:

- connect to an OBD-II adapter;
- display live vehicle information and diagnostic results;
- save vehicle profiles, preferences, scans, and service history on your device;
- unlock purchases and restore entitlements;
- provide optional PID, trouble-code, and catalog updates;
- diagnose technical problems and respond to support requests; and
- protect the App, users, and our services from misuse or security threats.

We do not use vehicle or diagnostic information for advertising or to build a profile about you.

### 5. Sharing and service providers

We do not share locally stored vehicle or diagnostic information unless you choose to export or submit it. Information may otherwise be handled by:

- **Apple**, for App Store distribution, purchases, refunds, entitlement verification, and related security;
- **GitHub**, when the App checks for or downloads optional data updates; and
- **Freshworks/Freshdesk**, when you choose to submit a support request.

These providers process information under their own privacy terms and security practices. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect legal rights or safety, or in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of the business, subject to applicable law.

### 6. Retention and deletion

Vehicle profiles, diagnostic sessions, custom PIDs, preferences, downloaded catalogs, and service history remain on your device until you delete them through available App controls, clear the App's data, or uninstall the App. AutoStack currently retains up to 20 diagnostic sessions and up to 100 service-operation records locally.

Support records are retained only as reasonably necessary to provide support, maintain business records, resolve disputes, prevent abuse, and satisfy legal obligations. You may request deletion of personal information submitted to support by using the contact method below. Apple, GitHub, and Freshworks control retention of information they process under their respective policies.

### 7. Your choices and rights

You can choose whether to create vehicle profiles, save a VIN, export diagnostic information, download updates, make purchases, or contact support. You can remove locally stored information using available App controls or by deleting the App.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold about you, or to object to or restrict certain processing. Submit a request through the support portal listed below. We may need to verify your request before acting on it.

### 8. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards appropriate to the information we handle. No device, network transmission, or storage system is completely secure. You are responsible for maintaining the security of your device, OBD-II adapter, local network, and exported files.

### 9. Children's privacy

AutoStack is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has submitted personal information to us, contact us so we can review and delete it where required.

### 10. International processing

Apple, GitHub, Freshworks, and their service providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Their privacy policies describe the safeguards applicable to their processing.
