MeshScan Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 25, 2026

MeshScan respects your privacy. The app does not collect, track, transmit, sell, or share personal information or usage data with its developer.

Data Processing and Storage
MeshScan processes scan data on your device. Temporary scans and related files are stored locally on your device. Scan files leave the app only when you choose to export or share them using features provided by iOS. The developer does not operate a server that receives your scans or other app data.

Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking
MeshScan does not include third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. The app does not create user accounts or build user profiles.

Apple Services
Apple may process information when you download or update MeshScan, make an optional in-app contribution, use Apple-provided sharing features, or choose to share device diagnostics with Apple. This processing is performed by Apple and is governed by Apple's privacy policies and your device settings. Apple may provide the developer with standard App Store sales, transaction, and aggregated diagnostic information, but MeshScan itself does not transmit personal information or scan data to the developer.

Children's Privacy
MeshScan does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

Changes to This Policy
This policy may be updated if MeshScan's features or data practices change. Any updated version will include a new effective date.

Contact
Questions about this privacy policy may be directed to the developer through the contact information provided on MeshScan's App Store listing.
