## Why We Update Frequently
You may have noticed that Vault Armory has been receiving frequent updates lately. We are actively working to improve the app, introduce new features, and refine the overall user experience.

Rather than holding improvements for larger releases, we prefer to make them available as they are ready.

## What's Next
Over the next couple of weeks, we will be focusing on additional security enhancements, performance optimizations, and improvements to existing features.

## Our Commitment to Privacy
Privacy and security remain at the core of Vault Armory. Our end-to-end encryption (E2EE) is designed to ensure that your encrypted personal records remain accessible only to you, not even we can access them.

We take privacy so seriously that we don't even know which individual users have subscribed or donated to support Vault Armory.

With that said, we want to personally thank everyone who has subscribed, donated, provided feedback, or simply continued to use the app. Your support helps fund ongoing development, infrastructure, and security improvements.

We remain committed to building a secure, reliable, and privacy-focused platform without compromising your personal information.

Thank you for supporting Vault Armory!
